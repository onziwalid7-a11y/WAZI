# WAZI — Wear Your Story

WAZI is a second-hand fashion marketplace where you can buy and sell stylish pre-owned clothing, shoes, and unique fashion pieces at affordable prices.

## What is WAZI?

WAZI gives great clothes a second life. Discover unique fashion pieces, sell items you no longer need, and make second-hand fashion more accessible.

## Features

- 🛍️ Buy second-hand clothing and shoes
- 💰 Sell your pre-owned items
- 🔎 Search for products
- 🏷️ Browse by category
- 🛒 Add products to your cart
- 📱 Responsive design for mobile and desktop
