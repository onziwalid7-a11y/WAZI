What is WAZI?

WAZI is a Moroccan second-hand fashion marketplace for pre-loved clothing, shoes and unique fashion pieces. Explore Men, Women and Children collections, discover vintage and quality used clothes, or sell pieces you no longer wear. WAZI connects fashion lovers in Marrakech, Ifrane and across Morocco.
