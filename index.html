Our idea

What is WAZI?

WAZI is a Moroccan second-hand fashion marketplace built around a simpler fashion experience: clear listings, useful fashion filters, direct contact, transparent delivery choices and a clean interface without intrusive marketplace advertising. Explore Men, Women and Children collections, discover pre-loved clothes and shoes, or sell pieces you no longer wear. WAZI connects fashion lovers in Marrakech, Ifrane and across Morocco.
